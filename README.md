## About me

I’m a QA Engineer and certified QA Analyst with a background in industrial engineering. I test web and mobile applications, validate APIs, and use SQL to check how business rules are reflected in the data.

I like to follow a user action through the whole workflow. In Nomad Pizza, for example, changing an ingredient should update the price on the menu, in the cart, and in the order summary. I turn checks like these into repeatable tests with Python, Selenium, and pytest, and document defects so others can reproduce them.

My experience in process auditing and Lean also shapes how I work in AutoCAD. For industrial piping projects, I write AutoLISP routines to generate labeled 3D components, export labels for inventory, and manage layers. I look for repetitive steps where automation can save work and reduce the chance of errors.
